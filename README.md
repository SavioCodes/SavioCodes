<h2>⚖️ Engineering Creed (How I Work)</h2>

<blockquote>
  <p>⚙️ <strong>Explainability Under Friction:</strong> I design systems to fail gracefully and be easily diagnosed when auth, queues, or third-party APIs break.</p>
  <p>📝 <strong>Operational Documentation:</strong> A feature isn't complete until its runbooks, trade-offs, and setup scripts are fully documented for operators.</p>
  <p>🛡️ <strong>Production over Hype:</strong> I prioritize deterministic, bounded system behavior over fragile, flash-in-the-pan AI demos.</p>
</blockquote>
